About the Competition

A legacy built on knowledge and clarity.

The Annual Maroof Raza Memorial Essay Writing Competition 2026, organised by the Maroof Raza Centre for Security Studies (MRCSS) & IIRIS Consulting, is a tribute to the enduring legacy of Maroof Raza one of India's foremost strategic affairs experts, respected national security commentator, and a valued advisor to IIRIS Consulting.

The competition aims to encourage young minds to think critically about the evolving security landscape and contribute fresh ideas on issues of national and global importance. It provides a platform for participants to showcase their research, analytical thinking, and writing skills while engaging with contemporary challenges in security, geopolitics, defence, technology, and public policy.

Whether you aspire to build a career in strategic affairs, defence, diplomacy, intelligence, cybersecurity, law, public policy, or research this competition offers an opportunity to share your perspectives and be recognised on a national platform.
