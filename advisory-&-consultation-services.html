IIRIS CONSULTING

Our Services

At IIRIS, we go beyond traditional consulting. As your risk advisory partner, we enable confident decision-making by integrating strategy, governance, and execution support at every stage. Our advisory services are designed to strengthen your organisational resilience, ensure regulatory readiness, and embed security by design.

01

Enterprise Risk Intelligence & ERM Advisory

IIRIS designs and operates risk-management frameworks that connect business objectives, operational indicators and decision rights. We structure enterprise risk registers, issue workflows, control libraries and management dashboards so that risks can be captured consistently, escalated efficiently and reported with board-ready clarity.

02

Crisis Preparedness & Response Design

We design crisis playbooks, escalation matrices, contact protocols and communications workflows that allow distributed teams to act with control under pressure. Table-top simulations, role-based drills and after-action reviews test decision paths before an incident occurs, while clear service-level expectations improve hand-offs across corporate, KPO and BPO operations.

03

Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Operations

IIRIS translates governance requirements into practical operating workflows. We map obligations to policies, controls, evidence requirements, owners and review cycles; maintain issue and action logs; and create reporting that gives management a reliable view of control performance. This helps regulated and high-growth organisations move from periodic compliance exercises to a disciplined, measurable GRC operating model.

04

Security Strategy, Architecture & Control Design

IIRIS helps clients define security architectures that link people, process, technology and governance. Our work covers threat-led design, operating procedures, control standards, command and monitoring workflows, technology requirements and vendor-neutral architecture reviews. The result is a scalable security model that is designed around business operations, not isolated products.
