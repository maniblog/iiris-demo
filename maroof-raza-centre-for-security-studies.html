MAROOF RAZA CENTRE FOR SECURITY STUDIES

Carrying Forward a Legacy of Strategic Clarity

The Maroof Raza Centre for Security Studies is established in memory of Late Maroof Raza, to ensure the conversations he championed continue with the same rigour, the same seriousness, and the same long-term national purpose.

An IIRIS Initiative

1959 — 2026
IN MEMORIAM

Maroof Raza

He brought intellectual sharpness without theatrics, conviction without noise, and patriotism anchored in responsibility.

Maroof Raza was one of India's most authoritative voices on defence and geopolitical affairs, a former officer of the Indian Army, and a scholar whose intellectual journey began at Mayo College, Ajmer, where he served as Head Boy of the 1975 Batch, before continuing at St. Stephen's College, Delhi, King's College London, and the University of Cambridge. As a broadcaster, his clarity and credibility on Times Now became a benchmark for serious security discourse.

He served in the Grenadiers and Mechanised Infantry, taught at the Indian Military Academy, and lectured at the NDA, NDC, and LBSNAA. His pioneering documentary series Line of Duty entered the Limca Book of Records. As publisher of SALUTE to the Indian Soldier, he ensured the stories of India's armed forces reached the widest possible audience.

As a Strategic Risk Advisor and Member of the Board of Advisors at IIRIS Consulting, and through the India Risk Report on ET Now, he championed the cause of national and corporate preparedness, bringing together leaders from industry and government to examine the risks shaping India's future. His contributions were not episodic. They were sustained, principled, and irreplaceable.

ABOUT THE CENTRE

Institutionalising a Vision.

01

As an Advisory Leader at IIRIS, his contribution went beyond counsel. He helped shape a way of thinking, one that approached risk with responsibility and strategy with foresight. This Centre is a step toward institutionalising that vision.

It serves as a platform for research, dialogue, and engagement on issues of geopolitical risk, national security, and emerging global challenges, building credible, enduring institutions that nurture the next generation of strategic thinkers.

OUR VISION To build a credible and enduring platform that advances strategic thinking, strengthens risk awareness, and nurtures future leaders in security and geopolitical affairs.

IIRIS Scholarships for PhD Scholars

The Centre invests in the next generation of strategic thinkers through the IIRIS Scholarships programme, with a flagship commitment to sponsor one PhD scholar annually, dedicated to research that carries forward the rigour and purpose that defined Maroof Raza's life's work.

Open to students with a keen interest in security and geopolitical affairs, the competition challenges young minds to analyse, argue, and articulate their perspective on the geopolitical situations shaping our world. A platform to think seriously, write sharply, and be heard.

WHY THIS MATTERS

In an uncertain world, clarity is not optional.

In an increasingly uncertain world, the ability to understand, anticipate, and respond to risk is no longer optional; it is essential.

Maroof Raza consistently advocated for deeper awareness, sharper thinking, and more responsible discourse around security and strategy. This Centre, and its initiatives, are built to ensure that its approach continues to inform how institutions and individuals engage with these challenges.

Some men fill a room when they walk in. And then some men fill the room even after they are gone. Maroof Raza was the latter.
