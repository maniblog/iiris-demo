Operational Resilience, Engineered for Scale.

IIRIS designs Business Continuity Management Systems that make resilience operational. We map critical processes, service dependencies, recovery requirements and escalation paths across corporate functions and distributed delivery teams. The resulting BCMS aligns governance, technology and people so that organisations can maintain service quality, manage disruption and demonstrate continuity readiness to clients, regulators and leadership.

IIRIS CONSULTING

Our Services

At IIRIS, we go beyond traditional consulting. As your risk advisory partner, we enable confident decision-making by integrating strategy, governance, and execution support at every stage. Our advisory services are designed to strengthen your organisational resilience, ensure regulatory readiness, and embed security by design.

01

Business Impact & Operational Dependency Analysis

IIRIS analyses critical activities, process hand-offs, systems, suppliers, sites and skills to show how disruption could affect business and client delivery. Structured workshops and scenario modelling establish recovery objectives, acceptable downtime, minimum-service requirements and practical priorities for restoration.

02

Testing, Simulation & BCMS Assurance

IIRIS validates plans through role-based exercises, scenario simulations and maturity reviews. We assess whether documentation is usable, responsibilities are understood and hand-offs work across locations and shifts. Findings are translated into corrective actions, ownership and follow-up reporting, turning testing into a continuous assurance cycle.

03

BCMS Framework, Governance & Workflow Design

IIRIS builds BCMS frameworks around governance, accountable owners, decision thresholds, document control and workflow discipline. We create a practical framework for leadership, operations, technology and client-service teams, with versioned records and review cycles that support scale without making the programme difficult to maintain.

04

Continuity, Recovery & Service Restoration Planning

IIRIS develops clear continuity and recovery plans for operations, technology, suppliers, people and communications. Plans define restoration sequences, alternate delivery arrangements, approvals, client communications and recovery evidence so teams can restore priority services with a common operating picture.
