IIRIS CONSULTING

Our Services

At IIRIS, we go beyond traditional consulting. As your risk advisory partner, we enable confident decision-making by integrating strategy, governance, and execution support at every stage. Our advisory services are designed to strengthen your organisational resilience, ensure regulatory readiness, and embed security by design.

01

Brand Intelligence & Marketplace Monitoring

IIRIS combines digital and market intelligence to identify suspected counterfeits, misleading listings, trademark misuse and channel-level anomalies. Monitoring can span online platforms, retail environments, wholesale markets and other agreed channels. Findings are captured in a structured case record with source references, visual evidence, severity assessment and recommended next steps, enabling client teams to prioritise review and response.

02

Evidence & Case-Readiness Support

IIRIS prepares well-organised intelligence packs for client legal and compliance teams. These may include evidence chronologies, source records, marketplace observations, research summaries and data analysis. The service supports internal review and case preparation while leaving legal advice, filing decisions and enforcement actions to the client and its appointed counsel.

03

Threat Analytics & Strategic Intelligence

IIRIS converts case observations and monitoring data into concise management intelligence. Periodic reports identify recurring patterns, emerging channels, product-category exposure and changes in risk severity, helping brand leaders focus prevention budgets, update monitoring priorities and brief internal stakeholders with confidence.
